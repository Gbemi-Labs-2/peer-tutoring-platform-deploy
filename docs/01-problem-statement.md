# Problem Statement

## Context

Students frequently need help understanding course material, preparing for
assessments, or finding a reliable study partner. Existing options can be
difficult to discover, inaccessible, costly, or disconnected from the
student's own institution and campus community.

Meanwhile, many competent students are willing to volunteer their time and
knowledge but do not have an organized, central platform through which they
can offer structured peer tutoring.

## Need

The campus community needs a trusted way to:

- connect students seeking academic support with compatible study partners
  and peer tutors;
- make qualified, willing peer tutors easy to discover;
- coordinate agreements and study-session schedules in one place; and
- support free, localized knowledge-sharing within the institution.

## Proposed solution

Develop a decentralized peer-to-peer academic support platform where verified
peer tutors match with students for free academic assistance. The platform
supports tutor discovery, compatibility matching, agreements, and scheduling
digitally. Knowledge delivery takes place through in-person, localized
communication on campus.

## Intended outcome

The platform should strengthen collaborative learning within the campus
community by making academic assistance more accessible while giving
volunteer tutors a clear and structured way to support their peers.
