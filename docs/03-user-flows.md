Here's the user flow in two parts. The first covers sign-up through to the session, and the second covers the review and feedback loop. Once the session starts, both sides follow the same path. This is where reviews and feedback come in, and the ratings feed back into future pairing.

Review and feedback loop
<img width="675" height="518" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/18f692fb-2dd2-42d9-b179-105c273f03bf" />

*How the feedback step works:* after a session is marked completed, the learner rates each tutor on that course. Scores cover teaching clarity, preparedness, punctuality and helpfulness, plus an optional written review. The ratings update the tutor's per-course rating status, and the review appears on their profile. Both users' histories are updated, and better-rated tutors rank higher when the system pairs people next time.
