Based on the survey data, responses are synthesized into two distinct user personas. These personas represent the most prominent patterns found among respondents, who are primarily university students looking to bridge gaps in their learning, stay consistent, and find reliable peers or tutors.

Persona 1: The Accountable Match-Seeker

"I understand things okay on my own, but distractions and poor time management derail me. I need an equal partner to stay disciplined."
                     ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
                     │ NAME: Sarah                             │
                     │ ACADEMIC LEVEL: 300 Level               │
                     │ PERFORMANCE: Good / Average             │
                     └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
• Demographics & Context: Typically a 200 to 300-level student in highly demanding fields like Computer Science or Engineering (e.g., OAU). They currently study alone or rely heavily on casual class group chats and coursemates.
• Core Challenges & Frustrations:
	• Distractions & Inconsistency: Struggle heavily with staying consistent and managing time effectively when studying in isolation.
	• Unreliable Peers: Express strong concerns about matching with uncommitted partners or encountering fake profiles.
• Needs & Goals:
	• Wants to maintain a strong grade point average and build absolute academic confidence.
	• Looks for a "Serious and committed attitude" above everything else, combined with shared courses and matching schedules.
• App Preferences:
	• Matching Method: Strictly automated based on their actual university timetable and availability.
	• Key Features: Calendar and scheduling tools, in-app messaging, automated study reminders, and zero-distraction modes.
	• Pricing: Strongly prefers a completely free app platform.

Persona 2: The Struggling Learner (Seeking Peer Tutoring)

"Some topics are just too difficult to digest alone. I want a trusted peer who can break complex ideas into clear chunks without costing me a fortune."

                     │ NAME: Emmanuel                          │
                     │ ACADEMIC LEVEL: 200 - 400 Level         │
                     │ PERFORMANCE: Average / Below Average     │
                     └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
• Demographics & Context: A student who frequently faces high-friction courses or feels left behind by regular lecture paces. They rarely or only occasionally study with others because they do not have an active study partner.
• Core Challenges & Frustrations:
	• Conceptual Roadblocks: Deep difficulty understanding certain technical topics or specific curriculum materials.
	• Fear of Hidden Costs: Highly worried about the potential cost of tutoring and running into unqualified tutors.
• Needs & Goals:
	• Wants to bridge the gap between their current level of understanding and high-competence students.
	• Looks for a partner or tutor who explicitly "explains concepts clearly" and possesses proven subject knowledge.
• App Preferences:
	• Trust Metrics: Needs verified student profiles, ratings/reviews, or a short introductory session to trust a tutor.
	• Key Features: Matching by course/subject, shared study notes, 1-on-1 online video tutoring, and repository spaces for PDFs or educational links.
	• Pricing: Prefers free access or a strictly budget-friendly "pay-per-tutoring-session" model.
